LegendArea names are the game’s own, as on the map opened with M in the game. Diamonds are entrances (cave mouths, stairs, portals, connecting passages): click one to go there. Where several floors share one picture, each door says which floor it leads to and a dashed line joins the two ends; thick ochre lines are stairs you can walk straight across. Purple marks bosses, yellow “!” quest characters, orange squares chests that give quest items. Grey see-through parts belong to a neighbouring teleport area: click to open that map; paler ones that cannot be clicked are areas you cannot walk to from here. Every entrance was tested in game (Google Play version): hollow dots are arrival points (where you land coming from elsewhere, not doors; marked “one-way” when there is no door back on this side); grey doors are missing on mobile or cannot be entered; red ones end the game (chapter ending); doors tagged “condition” open only once the condition shown above is met.
