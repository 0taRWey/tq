Change log

Version 2.3.7 (2026-11-12)
  - Skill text corrections: 1) Neidan’s Consequences: whenever you hit a target (normal attacks, skills, retaliation and damage reflection all count), it puts its debuff on that target, at the skill’s own level; the old “Casts (level 11)” and radius were wrong — that radius belongs to Mutual Suffering’s spread on death. The resistance reductions are unchanged. 2) Nature’s Refresh and the same skill on items: the old “refreshes skills with a cooldown of up to X s” now reads that the remaining recharge of every learned skill goes down by X s, long ones included; skills granted by items are not affected.
  - “Search everything” at the top also finds items by the skill they grant: e.g. “Blade Storm” finds Mantle of the Medjai, “Power Strike” finds Nidbow; the result notes which skill matched.

Version 2.3.6 (2026-11-12)
  - Item and set attribute lines now carry a tag right after them: lines in a “one of” group that never occur are marked “never occurs”, a line that is always the one picked is marked so, and uneven picks show each line’s share (e.g. Maron's Garment 25% / 75%); Instant Poison Damage is marked “no Dexterity scaling; +% Poison Damage does not apply”, the Base Instant Poison of Pearlstaff of Shen “scales with Dexterity”, and Poison Retaliation “instant poison, dealt once”. The note under the attributes is cut down to a sentence or two.
  - Lindane’s Koala Strike note rewritten without the unsupported “confirmed by player testing”.

Version 2.3.5 (2026-11-12)
  - Item, relic / charm and set details now have a “Read before use” section. 1) Instant poison: Instant Poison Damage added by gear does not scale with Dexterity and is not raised by “+% Poison Damage” (poison over time only); only the Base Instant Poison Damage of Pearlstaff of Shen scales with Dexterity; Poison Retaliation is instant poison dealt once; the “+50% Instant Poison Damage” on Scarab Ring is never picked; and which attacks carry instant poison from a weapon or from armor and jewelry. 2) “X% chance for one of the following”: the game weights each line by its own chance and, when no line has a chance, always takes the first one in the order it reads them. On 31 items (Legend of Fu Xi set included) some lines never occur or the pick is not even; each now says what actually happens and how often, e.g. Fafnir's Teeth is always pierce, Zheng Eight Snake Spear always gives its instant poison when the group triggers, Maron's Garment is 25% stun retaliation and 75% poison retaliation.

Version 2.3.4 (2026-11-12)
  - Build simulator · mastery skills: summon skills now carry two tags. The first says whether the summon counts as your pet. Pets (Battle Standard, Ancestral Horn, Summon Core Dweller, Summon Wisp, Lay Trap, Monster Lure, Call of the Wild, Sylvan Nymph, Circle of Power, Summon Liche King, Summon Outsider, Summon Nightmare, Dream Image, Terracotta Servants) are registered as yours: monsters they kill on their own give you experience and Electrum. Non-pets (Unyielding Phalanx, Briar Ward, Menhir Wall, Guardian Stones, summons placed as a ring or a row) give experience but no Electrum for their kills. The second tag says whether pet bonuses from items, affixes and skills reach the summon, which depends on the creature's own type: Dream Image (monster type), Unyielding Phalanx (guard type), Briar Ward and Menhir Wall (barriers) do not get them; Guardian Stones give no Electrum but are pet type, so pet bonuses apply; all the others get them.

Version 2.3.3 (2026-11-11)
  - Build simulator · gear slots: the prefix and suffix lists now filter each other. Before, with Durin's affixes (and loot affixes without the availability grid), choosing a suffix first did not narrow the prefix list, so prefixes that cannot come with it were listed and picking one cleared the suffix (e.g. Durin on an amulet: “of the Mammoth” first, “Veteran's” still listed; the amulet / ring / armband table has no such pair). Now whichever side you choose first, the other lists only what can come with it.

Version 2.3.2 (2026-11-11)
  - Build simulator · main skill: spells, including spells granted by items, now play their whole cast animation at cast speed; attack speed no longer matters. Tested in game (Google Play 3.0.5364): 19 mastery spells for both sexes at two speeds, and 46 of 47 item spells within 0.05 s of this, e.g. Ice Shard about 0.76 s at 100% cast speed; the page used to time the part after the release by attack speed, making the cast too long (about 1.1–1.3 s) and the DPS too low.
  - Build simulator · main skill: an item skill triggered on attack (e.g. Folg's Thunderclap) now triggers as in the game: no roll during its cooldown, then on average a further wait until it succeeds. Tested in game: Folg 5.5% chance, 8 s cooldown, about 1.1 attacks per second, 12 triggers in 304.8 s, i.e. 0.039 per second; now 0.041, before 0.060. Hits of the triggered skill itself roll again; that is not counted and the notes say so.
  - Build simulator · main skill: finale-charged skills (Calculated Strike, Psionic Touch, Huangdi's Favor) now average a cycle of normal attacks plus one finale for their DPS; the per-cast damage still shows the finale. Tested in game: Calculated Strike and Psionic Touch are 3 normal attacks + 1 finale; the page counted every attack as a finale, about twice the DPS. Psionic Beam's shorter charge is included.
  - Build simulator · main skill: Rune Weapon's own intelligence bonus at full charge (+100% at level 10) now counts for its damage. Tested in game: intelligence 1120 becomes 2240 at full charge and the lightning damage rises with it; the page left it out and the lightning was too low (the same lightning: page 71.6, game 127.3).
  - Build simulator · main skill: skills and gear that reduce the target's current health (Slam, Take Down, Seal of Fate, Shen Pao, Blade Barrier, and that line on gear) now show the percentage on its own line. It takes a share of the target's health at that moment and depends on its resistances, so it is not added to the per-cast or DPS figures; the notes say so. The page did not show it at all before (tested in game: all five skills take that share of the target's health).
  - Build simulator · main skill: Eruption, Blade Barrier and Lightning Bolt hit the same target several times; the notes now say the page counts one hit and that the real number depends on distance and the target's size (tested in game, one target at close range: Eruption 94 hits in 4 casts, Blade Barrier 91).

Version 2.3.1 (2026-11-11)
  - Build simulator · main skill: a skill granted by an item no longer has its recharge shortened by the character's recharge reduction. In the game item skills ignore it (tested in game: Shadowsting's Phantom Strike recharges in 16 s with or without recharge-reduction gear); the page used to make their recharge shorter and their DPS higher.
  - Build simulator · main skill: 21 item-granted weapon attacks are now offered (War Wind, Phantom Strike, Lethal Strike, Ternion Attack, e.g. Myrmidon's Pendant, Shadowsting, Thyestes' Scheme, Deep Sea Staff). These skills only carry % bonuses and were left out before. The weapon-pool skills granted by 15 items (Volley, Reckless Offense) now count toward the chance that a normal attack turns into a weapon-pool skill. Item-granted skills also check the weapons they require.
  - Build simulator: negative recharge reduction (Wrath of Hadad, Gem of Dione) no longer cancels another item's recharge reduction. The game adds each item's recharge reduction to the total only when it is above 0 (tested in game: Wrath of Hadad worn with a 9% item still shows 9%); the page used to add them up first and could end at 0%.
